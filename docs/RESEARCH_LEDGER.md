## Entry 002, examination structure

- Date researched: 08 September 2026
- Classification: OFFICIAL FACT
- Source: September 2026 Information Bulletin, section 7
- Finding: All CTET questions are MCQs with four alternatives, one most appropriate answer, one mark each, with no negative marking.
- Finding: Paper I is for intended teachers of classes I to V. Paper II is for intended teachers of classes VI to VIII. Candidates intending both levels must appear in both papers.
- Finding: Paper I has five compulsory components, each 30 MCQs and 30 marks, total 150 MCQs and 150 marks.
- Finding: Paper II has Child Development and Pedagogy, Language I and Language II at 30 MCQs each, plus either Mathematics and Science at 60 MCQs or Social Studies/Social Science at 60 MCQs, total 150 MCQs and 150 marks.
- Evidence: P8 lines 263-295; P8-P9 lines 302-322.

## Entry 004, scoring and qualification

- Date researched: 08 September 2026
- Classification: OFFICIAL FACT
- Source: September 2026 Information Bulletin, sections 7, 9 and 11
- Finding: Each question carries one mark. No negative marking.
- Finding: A person scoring 60% or more is considered TET pass under the cited NCTE notification.
- Finding: The CTET qualifying certificate is stated to have lifetime validity for all categories. There is no restriction on number of attempts, and qualified candidates may reappear to improve their score.
- Evidence: P8 lines 263-265; P9 lines 356-364; P10 lines 381-384.

## Entry 005, examination schedule and workflow facts

- Date researched: 08 September 2026
- Classification: OFFICIAL FACT
- Source: September 2026 Information Bulletin
- Finding: Main exam date stated is 06 September 2026. The bulletin states an additional 05 September 2026 date could be used if candidate numbers increase.
- Finding: Paper II is scheduled 09:30 AM to 12:00 noon and Paper I 02:30 PM to 05:00 PM, each 2.5 hours.
- Finding: Candidates are instructed to report 120 minutes before the start, 07:30 AM for Paper II and 12:30 PM for Paper I. Reporting after the examination start time is not permitted.
