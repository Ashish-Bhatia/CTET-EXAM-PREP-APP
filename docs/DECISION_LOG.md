# Decision Log

## D001, use CTET September 2026 bulletin as current-cycle baseline

- Date: 08 September 2026
- Classification: OFFICIAL FACT / PRODUCT DECISION
- Decision: Use the official CTET September 2026 Information Bulletin as the current examination specification baseline.
- Rationale: The official CTET website currently lists the September 2026 bulletin and current September 2026 notices. Earlier bulletins remain historical references.
- Source: https://ctet.nic.in/document/ctet-sept-2026-information-bulletin/

## D002, preserve evidence classification

- Date: 08 September 2026
- Classification: PRODUCT DECISION
- Decision: Every examination claim in project documentation must be tagged as OFFICIAL FACT, HISTORICAL OBSERVATION, ANALYTICAL ESTIMATE, PRODUCT DECISION, ASSUMPTION or UNVERIFIED.
- Rationale: Prevents derived analysis and product behaviour from being mistaken for CBSE rules.
